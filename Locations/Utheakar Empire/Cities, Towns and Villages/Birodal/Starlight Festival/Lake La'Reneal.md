This beautiful lake is located in the central park campus of the Centranade. It is a lake that spans the majority of the central hub of the park, and a network of bridges link each of the external locations of the Centranade. It is an ever flowing spring that feeds the waterfalls that coast off of the edges of the Centranade, and is a sparkling surface that attracts the eyes of all visitors of the lake. The network of bridges are made from a combination of ivory colored woods and stones, which intertwine to almost look hap-hazard but ultimately while walking make for easy traversing between the different areas. 

The water itself is clear as day, while the lake itself at some points reaches almost 50 feet deep you can see the bottom with astonishing clarity. It is lined with beautiful coy fish, and colorful corals.
