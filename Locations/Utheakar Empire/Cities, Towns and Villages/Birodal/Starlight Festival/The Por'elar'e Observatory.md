This observatory is one of, if not the most advanced and well maintained observatories in the entire known world. It is massive by size, the main body of it being constructed out of rich colored stones and dark mahogany woods. It's sturdiness seems to embody the strength and trust this society holds in its knowledge of everything, including the stars themselves.

While on any other occasion, the Observatory would be just another studying and knowledge based location in this campus, instead it has now been dressed for all sorts of celebrations. It's outside has been strewn in a smattering of lights and globules that make it light up like some sort of weird round Christmas tree.

Sticking out of the top is a pair of massive telescopes, both pointing in opposite directions. The importance of this observatory is that it is known for having a one of the only duel telescopes. Naturally this would be completely useless, but by use of the enchantment magic known well to this part of the world, they have managed to make a system allowing people to literally enter the constellations themselves through a stabilized pseudo-astral projection.
