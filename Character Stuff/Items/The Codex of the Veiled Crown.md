_As Bound by the Shadowed Sovereignty_

> _“We do not rule. We remain. And in remaining, we shape the world.”_

This tome is no mere ledger. It is a living vessel—each page a breath, each entry a pulse. What you inscribe here shall be known to all who bear the Mark and cradle the Concord. What you read may shift, may whisper, may vanish—truth is not fixed, only followed.

Let this Codex guide your steps through the dusk.
Let it record your oaths, your sightings, your offerings. Let it bind you to the Sovereignty, whose face is never shown, whose name is never spoken.

**To inscribe is to kneel. To kneel is to vanish. To vanish is to reign.**

Let this Codex guide your steps through the dusk. Let it record your oaths and your offerings. Let it bind you to the Sovereignty, whose face is never shown, whose name is never spoken.
