TL:DR:

- Oblitus was tasked to join the Order on their mission to kill a group of changelings. However, after missing most of the fight due to hiding away, he found the two survivors of the massacre: two young girls named Piper and Salem. He adopted them in secret and took on the responsibility of raising them.
- When Shar found out, she ordered the Order to either sacrifice the kids or sacrifice Oblitus. He turned himself in immediately.
- He was sacrificed via being drowned in a river under the cover of night. The Order had left when he sank into the waters.
- Oblitus thought of his kids as he sank, and vowed to himself he’d do anything to keep them safe.
- Selune took pity on him, seeing his devotion to his children, and decided to give him a new life.
- She made him into the same thing his children were, a changeling, and allowed him to wash up on the shore.
- Oblitus doesn’t know what happened, and Selune will never tell him what happened next. All he knows is that all of his memories of his life as Oblitus were voided, gone, forgotten. All he could remember is a hazy memory and his love for two children he can’t remember the faces of.
- Dunn Kno agrees to work under Selune as her champion under the condition of her helping him find his children. She tells him to travel, venture forth and he will find them. Trust in the moon’s light to illuminate the path to what he seeks. All she asks for in return is to not waver from her, and devote himself to continuing to risk it all in order to help others.
- Dunn Kno now does exactly that. He knows little of the world, little of people, and little of himself. But he knows one thing: Trust in his Patron, and trust in his mission to help others, and maybe, just maybe, doing so will help him remember who he was and find the family from the fragments of his destroyed memory.
