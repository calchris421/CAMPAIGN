TL:DR:

- Oblitus was tasked to join the Order on their mission to kill a group of changelings. However, after missing most of the fight due to hiding away, he found the two survivors of the massacre: two young girls named Piper and Salem. He adopted them in secret and took on the responsibility of raising them.
- When Shar found out, she ordered the Order to either sacrifice the kids or sacrifice Oblitus. He turned himself in immediately.
- He was sacrificed via being drowned in a river under the cover of night. The Order had left when he sank into the waters.
- Oblitus thought of his kids as he sank, and vowed to himself he’d do anything to keep them safe.
- Selune took pity on him, seeing his devotion to his children, and decided to give him a new life.
- She made him into the same thing his children were, a changeling, and allowed him to wash up on the shore.
- Oblitus doesn’t know what happened, and Selune will never tell him what happened next. All he knows is that all of his memories of his life as Oblitus were voided, gone, forgotten. All he could remember is a hazy memory and his love for two children he can’t remember the faces of.
- Dunn Kno agrees to work under Selune as her champion under the condition of her helping him find his children. She tells him to travel, venture forth and he will find them. Trust in the moon’s light to illuminate the path to what he seeks. All she asks for in return is to not waver from her, and devote himself to continuing to risk it all in order to help others.
- Dunn Kno now does exactly that. He knows little of the world, little of people, and little of himself. But he knows one thing: Trust in his Patron, and trust in his mission to help others, and maybe, just maybe, doing so will help him remember who he was and find the family from the fragments of his destroyed memory.

### Important things of Note: 

- Town: Waning Crest

- Son of the Dark Father Oberon and Dark Mother Ariel, senior members of the Order of the Dark Moon, and part of the Church of Shar 
    

  

### Timeline of Significant Events: 

- Born a twin in the family of two highly ranked sorcerers of the moon goddess, Shar’s, Order of the Dark Moon, an order of monks and sorcerers that worked in the Faerun. ORDER OF THE DARK MOON LORE BELOW
    

- The Order of the Dark Moon is a group within the Church of Shar, tasked to work directly with their goddess to carry out Shar’s will across the Realms. Their entire existence is dedicated to Shar and her dogma, seeking to commune and seek knowledge with shadows. 
    
- The Order believed that personal loss and the darkening of one's soul was the only means to find true insight into the spiritual self. They view themselves as saviors for knowledge by quenching the light of the moon by stealing Selune’s gifts to the world and fighting against those faithful to Selune. 
    
- The Order work via instilling fear in their devotees. Devotees would be punished if they were incapable to devote their life to Shar. Or they would be punished if they hide from the light and didn’t take action under the protection of the darkness. Or they could be punished by the priests if Shar deemed them guilty of misusing her source of magic. Or they’d be punished for simply acting out before the Order. 
    

- Now death as a punishment happens very rarely. It was typical to only potential recruits that were incapable of murder
    

- Those in the Order were sure to prepare their pupils of the perils or foes before going on missions. 
    

- Typically, Shar demanded the Order to never recruit children, as they only provide interference. However, when Ariel and Oberon came forward about their twins, and originally were going to accept punishment or dismissal from the Order for their interference, the Order of the Dark Moon sought this as an opportunity. 
    

- Ariel and Oberon were both ranked as seniors of the Order, given the titles of Dark Father Oberon and Dark Mother Ariel.
    
- Shar and Selune were twin goddesses, so having twins in the Order of the Dark Moon seemed like a blessing from Shar. This led the Order to not only allow their parents to stay, but also to recruit Oblitus and Abiit to be raised by the Order instead of the Church. 
    

- Oblitus and Abiit were viewed highly, and raised under close watch of the Order. Shar took favor on the twins, and often pitted them against each other due to her own jealousy with her sister. 
    

- As they grew up, the Order often had the two spar and practice their magic on one another. 
    
- Oblitus was skilled with magic, using the majority of his free time studying new spells and maneuvers. However his talent stemmed from melee when he actually fought. 
    
- Abiit was highly talented with her magic, but weak with melee fighting. She surpassed Oblitus in magic duels frequently. 
    
- However, due to Oblitus’ jack of trades, the Order took favor upon him.
    

- This sparked a deep envy within Abiit. 
    

- They worked under their parents in the Order, attending meetings and special events of the Church of Shar. They were placed on a pedestal by their Church, which filled Oblitus with immense anxiety, meanwhile, it filled Abiit with pride.

- He took them to an abandoned cabin for them to live, fixing it up and making it into a home for all three of them.

- He continued to find ways to avoid killing, with Abiit covering his cowardly ass every mission. 
    
- He was exhausted all the time, but did his best to stay attentive towards the needs of his kids and avoid the suspicions of his Church, and especially of his sister. 
    

- Eventually, Oblitus became too fatigued, causing him to slip up. He’d fall asleep during Order meetings, he’d sleep too long at the cabin and be late to mission briefings, he’d mess up and get hurt on missions causing the kids to get concerned when he’d arrive late and injured to the cabin. This double life was starting to catch up with him. 
    

- This progression of slip ups was being noticed by his sister, Abiit. Who became increasingly more suspicious of her brother’s actions. She dedicated it was time for an intervention.
    


# Oblitus’ Downfall

### Key Figures:

- Ariel (His mother)
    
- Oberon (His father)
    
- Abiit (his twin sister)
    
- Shar (His goddess)
    
- Salem (His eldest daughter) (roughly 11-12yrs old)
    
- Piper (His youngest daughter) (roughly 7-8yrs old)
    

  

### Important things of Note: 

- Abiit was suspicious of Oblitus, and cast modified memory to make Oblitus believe he has trusted her and that they were closer than they actually are. This causes him to slip up and mention the cabin and the girls.
    
- Abiit followed Oblitus to his cabin and sees he had lied to the Order all this time, she goes to report it to the Order
    
- The Order give Oblitus the choice: Sacrifice the kids, or sacrifice himself 
    
- Oblitus chooses to run and try to save the kids.
    

- He fails running, but the kids get away, promising to find each other again one day
    

- Oblitus is brutally sacrificed and unrecognizable when he is thrown into the water to be “cleansed” by the full moonlight 
    
- Salem and Piper are now out in the world looking for their father
    

  

### Timeline of Significant Events: 

-  Abiit had grown tired of being in her brother's shadow, especially when that shadow was constantly wavering and failing at his job. She started taking notice of his slipping away and disappearances, and decided to start following him. She started small, just seeing where he was going outside of the Church, watching as he left the town as a whole and disappeared past the tree line.
    

- The next time she followed, she reached the tree line with him, but he vanished from view once again. She realizes he’s been casting invisibility on himself, so she’d have to find his secret destination herself. 
    
- When she finds Oblitus exhausted in the Church, nodding off to an unplanned sleep, she realizes this is the best time to strike. 
    

- She cast modify memory, making him believe he has trusted her with this secret all along, adding a memory of them discussing where he goes. 
    
- When he wakes up and realizes he’s late to go back to the cabin, she asks him where he’s off to. Oblitus spills that he’s going to the cabin in the woods, but he needs to get going before they get worried about him again. 
    
- She lets him run this time.
    

- The next time, Abiit once again follows Oblitus, this time able to know what she should be looking for when he disappears. She follows the rustling of his footsteps until they come upon a cabin. 
    
- Invisible to Oblitus, she peeked into the home he had built for himself and his little family, seeing the two changeling children alive and well from the mission years prior. She recognized the toys the two played with as they sat on the floor beside her brother reading to them. 
    

- She felt betrayed by his deceit of the Order, lied to by her brother hiding this, saddened he wouldn’t include her in this life, envious that the only reason she lost the position she so deeply wanted was all because of a lie… then mischievous. The position was lost because of a lie. She could expose him and take his place. 
    
- Abiit ran off to report him to the Order, Oblitus hearing the rustling outside their window as she did. He told the girls to hide, and he made his way back to the Church. 
    

- Oblitus was too late, Abiit was faster than he was and was already telling their parents along with the rest of the seniors of the Order of the Dark Moon. 
    

- She confessed Oblitus had lied about his killings, that he was a coward, and not only that, but he had directly disobeyed the orders of Shar by taking into the children and raising them as his own. 
    

- Disobeying Shar’s orders was punishable by death. The Order stripped Oblitus of his rank immediately, but offered him an exchange for his life. 
    

- Oblitus could either give up the children, allowing them to be sacrificed to their goddess Shar as a repayment for his betrayal, or he could face the punishment himself. 
    

- Oblitus ran. 
    

- He ran fast, he ran back to the cabin, stuffing essentials into a bag and grabbing Salem & Piper, going to run away with them in tow. His mind raced, he figured he could take them to a nearby city, leave them as an orphanage or somewhere that would be willing to take them in, and he can run. He’d rather they get somewhere safe than continue to be in danger because of him. 
    

- The three ran through the woods, hoping to get far enough away that the Order would not catch up. 
    

- He was not fast enough, Abiit caught up, as did the rest of the Order. The deal had been denied, leaving them with the orders to kill all three of them. 
    

- When knocked down, horrified when he hears Piper shriek with fright when they’re about to be grabbed and taken from him, Oblitus cast mage hand to push the girls as far as he could and yelled at them to once again run, run away, hide yourselves, and he’d find them again, he promised he’d find them. 
    

- Salem protested, but when seeing Oblitus’ pleading eyes, she took Piper’s hand and ran, shouting back as she ran that she promises to find him too.  
    

- Oblitus fights off the Order as best he could, refusing to hold back. He was able to stand his ground longer than expected, but eventually was taken down, beaten near an inch of his life, and dragged away. 
    
- Oblitus was persecuted with the order of being sacrificed until the moonlight, and Abiit was present for the sacrifice along with their parents and the rest of the Order.
    

- As first she felt prideful in her loyalty to their goddess, but as she watched her brother dragged to the sacrificial stage beside the river’s rushing current along the Church’s edge, and as she looked as her brother’s heartbroken face, she couldn’t help but feel horrified by her own actions.
    
- Oblitus felt haunted by the stare of his family, but as he locked eyes with his sister, someone he thought he could trust, someone he had memories of trusting deeply enough with this part of his life, he felt his heart shatter. 
    
- After being beaten up, sliced up, and just about killed by the Order that raised him, he was practically unrecognizable. 
    

- Oblitus felt himself get thrown into the water, to be “cleansed” by the moonlight, as he remembered them stating when he witnessed these brutal rituals growing up. 
    
- The look of his sister standing over him burned into his memory as he sank deeper. 
    
- His eyes began to close as he looked up through the rippling image of the full moon shining down.

- Oblitus became a changeling. 
    

- There was one issue with this resurrection… upon making him a changeling, and solidifying his oath to her as a new Paladin of devotion to protect… separating him from Shar corrupted his memory, and Selune didn’t know how to repair it. 
    
- His soul was now without any memory, his body was now without a concrete form, Oblitus had become something else entirely, he became something unknown. 
    

- Selune felt guilt for her new paladin, lost in the waters and lost in himself. She swore to guide him, to help him with his oath even if he couldn’t remember it.
