## Worshipers[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar?veaction=edit&section=9 "Edit section: Worshipers")

_Main article: [Church of Shar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Church_of_Shar "Church of Shar")_

"Mother of Night, darken my step as I walk among the light. Hear my prayer.”

— A basic prayer to Shar.[[15]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-bg3-15)

People turned to Shar's dark embrace in order to forget, whether it was pain at the loss of a loved one or bitterness at a betrayal.[[1]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-CotR-p14-1)[[18]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-RtR-p48-18)[[6]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FaP-p58-6)[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-SCAG5e-p21,36-37-9) Those who were [blind](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Blind "Blind"), particularly blinded later in life by the cruelty or carelessness of others, would also pray to her for guidance. The mentally ill and the mad, and the anarchistic and the nihilistic, might also turn to her if they had a natural affinity for her ways.[[6]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FaP-p58-6) Those sunk deep in depression would also pray to Shar.[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-SCAG5e-p21,36-37-9) Though they all knew her to be dark and vindictive, they came to her anyway in the desperate belief that she and her clergy would give solace, not thinking they would use their feelings to corrupt them. Good priests, especially those of Selûne, Mystra, and Lathander, warned them away, but often in vain. In this way, the clergy of Shar grew.[[6]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FaP-p58-6) Ultimately, they were simply consumed by bitterness, loss, and vengefulness.[[8]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FRPG4e-p152-8) She was also venerated, or at last paid lip service, by beings who avoided or despised light, those who operated at night like thieves and killers, and those with something to hide or bury.[[1]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-CotR-p14-1)[[18]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-RtR-p48-18)

“Of course, there is no reason to try to hide anything from the Mistress of the Night—once one has realized this, and has opened one's self to her cold, comforting embrace, there is no going back... For the Dark Lady rewards loyalty with power beyond imagination.”

— [Oruana Drearon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Oruana_Drearon "Oruana Drearon")[[64]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-nwn2-65)

However, folk who only had to work at night or in the dark, such as miners, would offer prayers to Shar to keep dangers at bay. Even those who'd simply lost something might beg her help finding it.[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-SCAG5e-p21,36-37-9) So, she might be worshiped by people of any morality, whether [lawful](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lawful "Lawful") or [chaotic](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chaotic "Chaotic") or even [good](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Good "Good"), but most often by those of [evil](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Evil "Evil") nature,[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-F&A-p138-4)[[10]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-TWoN-p55-10)[[11]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-OHG-p170-11) such as villainous [thieves](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Thieves "Thieves").[[1]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-CotR-p14-1)

Although she was a [human](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Human "Human") deity, Shar was also commonly worshiped by [goblinoids](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Goblinoid "Goblinoid") and other creatures who abhorred light, whether it was because they lived underground or they acted at night. Either way, they paid her homage when working in the dark.[[2]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FRA-30-2)[[18]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-RtR-p48-18)[[6]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FaP-p58-6)

Under her stolen alias as Ibrandul, the Lurker in Darkness, Shar was also worshiped by the [Ibrandulin](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Ibrandulin "Ibrandulin"). She still granted spells to their priests.[[4]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-F&A-p138-4)

The clergy of Shar were a secretive organization that pursued subversive tactics rather than direct confrontation with its rivals. In many cities where worshiping Shar was barred, these churches and temples were hidden because of their illegal nature. In addition to her clerics, Shar maintained an elite order of [sorcerer](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sorcerer "Sorcerer") [monks](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Monks "Monks"), the [Order of the Dark Moon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Order_of_the_Dark_Moon "Order of the Dark Moon"), who could tap Shar's Shadow Weave.[[6]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FaP-p58-6)[[65]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-LoD-p122-66)

In the time of ancient [Netheril](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Netheril "Netheril"), Shar was among the deities venerated by the lower classes.[[66]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-67) Among her worshipers were the [Shadovar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shadovar "Shadovar") of [Thultanthar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Thultanthar "Thultanthar"), who fled into the Plane of Shadow before [Karsus's Folly](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Karsus%27s_Folly "Karsus's Folly") and devoted their whole society to her.[[67]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-RoF-p149-68)[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-SCAG5e-p21,36-37-9) In their restored Empire of Netheril in the 15th century, the Shadovar made worship of Shar the state religion and the basis for their law, with all other faiths forbidden.[[7]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FRCG4e-p76-7)[[68]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-69) Their fanaticism and tyranny, and their crimes for the glory of Shar, caused much loss and grief and led to widespread bans on her faith by the late 1400s.[[9]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-SCAG5e-p21,36-37-9)

Shar was also venerated by many of those who used the Shadow Weave.[[69]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-FRCS-p37-70)

### Heresies[](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar?veaction=edit&section=10 "Edit section: Heresies")

Those who believed in the [Dark Moon heresy](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Moon_heresy "Dark Moon heresy") believed that Shar and Selûne were two faces of the same goddess.[[70]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-PoF-p47-71)

The [She-Spider Cult](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/She-Spider_Cult "She-Spider Cult") of [Thay](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Thay "Thay") tried to combine the faiths of Shar and [Lolth](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lolth "Lolth"), but both goddesses ultimately rejected them, leaving them no divine aid.[[71]](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Shar#cite_note-72)
